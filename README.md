\# VBA-SCRAG



\*\*A Verify-before-Accept Framework for Reliability-Aware Self-Correcting Retrieval-Augmented Generation\*\*



\## Research Objective



VBA-SCRAG investigates whether verifying a correction candidate before accepting it can improve the reliability of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems.



\## Problem Statement



A correction generated by a RAG system is not necessarily better than its initial answer. This project investigates an acceptance gate that compares the grounding scores of the initial answer and correction candidate before deciding whether to accept the correction.



\## System Components



\- \*\*Retriever:\*\* Retrieves the top three evidence documents using `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`.

\- \*\*Answer Generator:\*\* Uses `google/flan-t5-small`.

\- \*\*Grounding Verifier:\*\* Calculates cosine similarity between the answer and retrieved evidence.

\- \*\*Corrector:\*\* Generates a candidate answer after adding a correction instruction to the question.

\- \*\*Acceptance Gate:\*\* Accepts a candidate when its grounding score improves by at least the configured margin.



\## Preliminary Evaluation



\- \*\*Dataset:\*\* HotpotQA distractor validation split

\- \*\*Pilot size:\*\* 50 questions

\- \*\*Evaluation metrics:\*\* Answer F1, Exact Match, semantic answer similarity, and grounding score

\- \*\*Comparison systems:\*\* Vanilla RAG, correction without a gate, and VBA-SCRAG



The paired experiment showed that the acceptance gate increased average grounding similarity compared with correction without a gate. However, Answer F1 and Exact Match were lower for the gated system. These findings indicate that embedding-based grounding similarity alone is insufficient to establish factual correctness.



\## Next Improvement Plan



1\. Investigate Natural Language Inference (NLI) for evidence-based verification.

2\. Explore combining evidence support with grounding-score improvement.

3\. Repeat controlled paired experiments and expand evaluation where feasible.

4\. Update the research paper based on the measured results.



\## Current Status



The initial implementation and pilot evaluation are complete. NLI-based verification and expanded evaluation are planned future work and have not yet been completed.
